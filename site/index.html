geodocOffline document AI for land-surveying reports

geodoc reads Polish land-surveying reports (operaty) and turns them into checked coordinate tables in Excel and CSV, plus a chapter map whose summaries point back to their pages.

It runs on one office machine: a single open model on llama.cpp, CPU only, no cloud, so the documents never leave the office.

Every value is checked against the document it came from and by rules written in code, and every result carries a seal that makes any later edit visible.
